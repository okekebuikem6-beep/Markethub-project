1. PERSONAL DATA CONTROLLER

MarketHub is responsible for determining how personal data collected through the Service is processed, subject to applicable data protection laws.

Depending on the services you use, certain third-party service providers may independently process personal data in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us and information that is automatically collected when you use the Service.

2.1. Data you give us

📝 Registration Information

When you register for or use the Service, we may ask you to provide information such as your name, telephone number, email address and other information required to create and maintain your account.

👤 Public Profile Information

When you publish an advertisement, you may choose to provide additional information about yourself, including photographs, descriptions and other information included in your listing.

Please carefully consider the information you choose to make publicly available through your advertisements.

2.2. Data provided by third parties

🔐 Social Login Services

If you choose to sign in using a third-party service such as Google, Apple or another supported authentication provider, we may receive information made available to us by that provider.

The information received may include your name, email address, profile image or an account identifier.

👥 Referrals

If another person invites you to use MarketHub, that person may provide us with information necessary to send you the invitation.

2.3. Data we collect automatically

🌐 Device and Location Data

We may collect information about the device you use to access the Service, including your IP address, browser, operating system, device type, language settings and related technical information.

📊 Usage Data

We may record information about how you interact with the Service, including the pages, features and advertisements you view and how frequently you use the Service.

💳 Transaction Data

When you make payments through the Service, payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers. MarketHub may receive information about the transaction, such as the date, amount and payment method.

🍪 Cookies and Similar Technologies

MarketHub may use cookies and similar technologies to improve the functionality, security and performance of the Service.

3. DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES

In handling personal data, MarketHub aims to ensure that personal data is:

  • processed for specific, lawful and legitimate purposes;
  • adequate, relevant and reasonably accurate;
  • retained only for as long as reasonably necessary; and
  • protected against reasonably foreseeable security threats and unauthorized access.

4.2. To enable secure access

We may process information to verify accounts, protect users and maintain the security of the Service.

4.3. To customize your experience

We may use information to personalize content and improve the relevance of the Service.

4.4. Customer support

We may process your information when responding to support requests, technical problems or other communications.

4.5. Communication

We may communicate with you through email, notifications, messages or other available communication channels.

4.6. Research and improvement

We may analyze how users interact with MarketHub in order to improve existing features and develop new services.

4.7. Marketing communications

Where permitted by law, we may send information about MarketHub products, services, promotions and offers.

4.8. Advertising

We may use information to provide more relevant advertising and measure advertising performance, subject to applicable law and available user choices.

4.9. Security and fraud prevention

We may process information to detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, unauthorized activity and violations of our Terms of Use.

4.10. Legal compliance

We may process or disclose information where required or permitted by applicable law.

4.11. Payments

We may use third-party payment providers to process transactions for paid products or services.

Depending on the circumstances, MarketHub may process personal data on the following legal bases:

5.1. Your consent

Where required, we may process your personal data based on your consent.

5.2. Performance of a contract

Processing may be necessary to provide the Service and perform our agreement with you.

5.3. Legitimate interests

We may process information where necessary for legitimate interests, including improving the Service, preventing fraud, communicating with users and protecting our rights.

5.4. Legal obligations

We may process personal data when required to comply with applicable legal obligations.

We may share information with third parties that help us operate, provide, improve, secure or support the Service.

6.1. Service providers

Depending on the services you use, these may include:

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Authentication providers
  • Marketing service providers
  • Email delivery providers
  • Payment service providers
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers
  • Technical support providers

6.2. Law enforcement and public authorities

We may disclose personal data when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, property or the security of the Service.

6.3. Corporate transactions

If MarketHub is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, sale of assets or similar transaction, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction where legally permitted.

If a personal-data breach occurs, MarketHub will take appropriate steps required by applicable data protection law, including notifying relevant authorities or affected individuals where notification is legally required.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection supervisory authority or seek appropriate legal remedies.

8. NO LIMITATION CLAUSE

Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to exclude or limit liability where such exclusion or limitation is prohibited by applicable law.

9. HOW YOU CAN EXERCISE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

Accessing and correcting your data

You may request access to personal information held about you and ask us to correct inaccurate information.

Deleting your personal data

You may request that your personal data be deleted, subject to legal requirements that may require us to retain certain information.

Restricting or objecting to processing

Where applicable, you may request that we restrict or stop certain processing of your personal data.

Data portability

Where applicable, you may have the right to receive your personal data in a structured and commonly used format.

Withdrawing consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

To exercise your privacy rights, contact the MarketHub support team through the contact information provided below.

MarketHub does not knowingly collect personal data from children where doing so is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal data to MarketHub in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Some of our service providers may process personal data outside Nigeria or outside the country where your data was originally collected.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, MarketHub will take appropriate measures required by applicable data protection laws.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If material changes are made, we may notify you through the Service or other appropriate communication channels.

Your continued use of the Service after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means that you acknowledge the revised policy.

We retain personal data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

When personal data is no longer required, we may delete, anonymize or otherwise securely dispose of it in accordance with applicable law.
